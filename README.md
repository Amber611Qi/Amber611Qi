### About me

I study how machine learning and physical knowledge can help recover and predict ocean conditions from incomplete satellite observations. My doctoral research focuses on **sea surface temperature (SST) reconstruction and short-term prediction**, with particular interest in preserving ocean structures under cloud contamination.

At the South China Sea Institute of Oceanology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, I work on methods that combine satellite data, statistical background fields, and physical priors. My broader interests include physics-aware learning, multi-source observation fusion, and AI for Earth observation.

### Research

| Focus | What I work on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reconstructing the ocean surface** | Spatiotemporal learning to recover cloud-affected SST fields from satellite observations. |
| **Combining data and physical knowledge** | Background-constrained residual learning and fusion of statistical and advection-based priors. |
| **From reconstruction to prediction** | Evaluating reconstructed SST for short-term forecasting across coastal and offshore environments. |
